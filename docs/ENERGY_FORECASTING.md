# StepCharge AI — Time-Series Energy Forecasting
## Autoregressive Yield Prediction, Temporal Splits & Anti-Fabrication Safeguards

---

### 1. Objective & Methodology

The **Energy Yield Forecaster** predicts the cumulative electrical energy (in Joules) generated by the footstep harvester over upcoming operational horizons (e.g. next 50 or 100 footsteps).

Unlike static linear extrapolation, the forecaster uses a **Random Forest Regressor** trained on sequential historical footsteps, capturing temporal dynamics such as:
- Human fatigue (gradual reduction in step impact force over time).
- Cadence bursts (periods of rapid footsteps followed by rest intervals).
- Supercapacitor back-EMF saturation (lower energy absorption as terminal voltage nears rated capacity).

---

### 2. Strict Temporal Validation (No Shuffling)

In time-series forecasting, standard random cross-validation represents **data leakage from the future into the past**. The StepCharge AI forecasting engine strictly enforces **chronological ordering**:

$$\text{Train} = \{ x_1, x_2, \dots, x_k \}, \quad \text{Test} = \{ x_{k+1}, \dots, x_N \}, \quad \text{where } t_1 < t_2 < \dots < t_N$$

Data shuffling is strictly prohibited.

---

### 3. Autoregressive Feature Construction

For each footstep at chronological index $t$, the feature engine derives:
1. $\text{energy}_{t-1}, \text{energy}_{t-2}, \text{energy}_{t-3}$: Prior energy yields.
2. $\text{peakVoltage}_{t-1}, \text{pulseDuration}_{t-1}$: Prior strike intensity.
3. $\Delta t_{t-1}$: Inter-step cadence duration.
4. $\text{rolling\_avg\_energy}$: Moving average over a 5-step rolling window.

---

### 4. Zero-Fabrication Safeguard ($N \ge 20$)

A key tenet of StepCharge AI Phase 3 is **Zero Data Fabrication**. If a device or test rig has generated fewer than 20 recorded sequential footsteps, the forecasting model refuses to construct speculative trendlines.

When $N < 20$:
- Endpoint returns `{ "isAvailable": false, "status": "INSUFFICIENT_DATA" }`.
- The user interface explicitly renders:
  ```
  [!] INSUFFICIENT HISTORICAL DATA FOR FORECASTING
  Energy forecasting requires at least 20 sequential footsteps collected
  under active telemetry to establish autoregressive lag features.
  Historical Samples Available: 12 / 20
  ```
- **Guaranteed Benefit**: Eliminates phantom charts and fabricated curves in academic and industrial demonstrations.
